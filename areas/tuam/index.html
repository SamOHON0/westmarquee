Parties for younger children

For a party of under tens, a themed castle or a course with a low age limit is the safe choice. All three of these suit children from age 2.

  • Disco Obstacle Course (44ft long, ages 2 to 14): tunnels, bish bash obstacles, climb walls and a big slide, suited to everyone from age 2 up to 14, with over 40 themes. It needs roughly 54ft of flat length once you add the clearance.
  • Combi Castle: Spiderman (16ft x 15ft, ages 2+): a full bouncing area and slide, and it suits boys and girls from toddlers up. It needs roughly 26ft by 25ft of flat ground once you add the clearance.
  • Combi Castle: Barbie (16ft x 15ft, ages 2+): bright pink with a full bouncing area plus a slide. It books out fast in summer, so get your date in early. It needs roughly 26ft by 25ft of flat ground once you add the clearance.

Before the day

Every inflatable needs a flat area big enough for the unit with at least 1.5 metres of clear space on all sides, clear access to the site, and a standard plug socket nearby for the blower. If you are unsure about your space, send us a photo on WhatsApp and we will tell you straight away.

Most of our castles have rain covers, so light rain is no problem. In high winds or severe weather we work with you to rearrange. Every unit is cleaned and inspected between hires, and we are fully insured for inflatable hire.
